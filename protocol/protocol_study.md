# Systematic Review Protocol

## Review Title
Machine learning models for predicting the risk of unplanned readmission and mortality in the ICU: A systematic review and meta-analysis.

## Research Question
In patients admitted to intensive care units, what differences in predictive performance are observed between artificial-intelligence-based model architectures that explicitly and jointly predict both unplanned ICU readmission and mortality within the same study?

## PICO Framework

### Population
Patients admitted to the Intensive Care Unit (ICU).

### Intervention
Predictive models based on artificial intelligence and machine learning applied to ICU patient data.

### Comparison
Different artificial-intelligence/machine-learning model architectures, within and across studies.

### Outcome
Simultaneous prediction of: unplanned ICU readmission and mortality.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Inclusion Criteria
Only studies that evaluate and report predictive performance for both unplanned ICU readmission and mortality within the same study are eligible. Studies that evaluate only one of these outcomes are excluded. Original research articles, published in English, in peer-reviewed journals between 2020 and 2025. Studies that do not include COVID-19 patients, to avoid bias arising from the clinical and epidemiological variability associated with the disease. Studies with external validation of the proposed model.

### Exclusion Criteria
Studies not related to machine learning or artificial intelligence; not focused on ICU patients; predicting outcomes other than unplanned readmission or mortality; reviews, editorials, case reports, conference abstracts, conference proceedings, non-peer-reviewed publications; and studies focused on pediatric ICU patients, coronary care units (CCU) or cardiac intensive care units (CICU), patients with acute heart failure, respiratory intensive care units, or surgical intensive care units. Cancer patients; studies including patients diagnosed with SARS-CoV-2. Articles with insufficient sample sizes or without external validation of the proposed models. Studies related to sepsis-related death.

## Registration and Public Availability

This systematic review was not prospectively registered in PROSPERO or any other registry. The protocol (search strategy, eligibility criteria, data extraction forms, and analysis plan) was documented before the manuscript was drafted and is made publicly available in this repository alongside the article's resubmission. To be precise: this repository was not made public until after the review was substantially conducted, so its public availability should not be read as preceding the review's conduct — the protocol existed in documented form and is now shared here in full alongside the manuscript. No amendments were made to the protocol relative to the analyses reported here.
